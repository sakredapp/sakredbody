# Store listing

The copy that goes in App Store Connect and the Play Console. Kept here
because a store listing is the only part of the product that ships without a
build, which makes it the only part with no history unless we give it one.

Both stores render **plain text**. No markdown, no HTML — a line break is a
line break and an asterisk is an asterisk. So the copy below is written the
way it should appear, and the headings are just short lines with a full stop,
not styled elements. Paste it as-is.

Do not hard-wrap the paragraphs when pasting. The stores reflow text to the
device width; a newline inside a sentence survives and reads as a broken line
on every phone.

## Description — both stores

Character count: **1,264** of 4,000 on each store.

```
Most health apps are built for everyone. This one isn't.

Sakred Body is a daily instrument for people who are serious about how they feel and have no intention of living like a monk to get there.

Rest and strength aren't opposites. Most people are running one at the cost of the other and can't tell which. The work is knowing what today is for, and then actually doing it.

A protocol, in order.
Liver, gut, lymph, sleep, training. Not a pile of habits someone assembled — a sequence, a reason for the sequence, and something to notice at each step.

A coach who reads it.
Not a support inbox. Not a chatbot. A person who sees what you tracked this week and writes to you about it. You're never more than one screen away.

A note every morning.
Short. Built from your own records. What you did, what's next, why it sits in that order.

The Apothecary.
The products a protocol actually asks for. What it's for, why this one, where it fits. Nothing padded out to fill a shelf.

Study and gather.
Guides and masterclasses tied to the protocol you're running, not floating in a library. Retreats and masterminds, booked in the app.

Sakred Body is a membership. An account is required to use the app.

Sakred Body is health education and coaching, not medical care.
```

### The last two lines are not boilerplate

Apple's guideline 2.1 requires an app behind a login to say so before download,
and both stores treat an undisclosed paywall as a rejection rather than a
correction. The medical line is the other half: everything this app says is
bounded by it, and a reviewer who reads "protocol" without it is reading a
claim we do not make.

Neither line should be trimmed to save space. There are over 2,700 characters spare.

## What is deliberately absent

No feature list, no screenshots described in words, no "AI-powered". The
description names five things and says what each is *not*, which is the whole
voice: a coach who reads it is defined against a support inbox and a chatbot,
and the Apothecary is defined against a shelf. Adding a sixth bullet weakens
the five.
